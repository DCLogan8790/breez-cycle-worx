Featured Projects

Real Work, Real Communities.

These started as trail conversations and turned into real concepts and completed work for communities around Western Kentucky. Here's a look at what we've been part of.

Start a Conversation
🗺️
Park Master Plan
Henderson Park Master Plan

Creating a long-term vision for outdoor recreation that benefits the entire community — layout concepts, feature placement, and a phased approach the city can act on.

🍃
Trail Initiative
Madisonville Trail Initiative

Strengthening connections through active transportation and sustainable trail development — helping the city build infrastructure that gets more people outside.

🚲
Cycling Program
Hopkins County Cycling

Supporting riders through education, maintenance, and community events — building a stronger local cycling culture county-wide.

🚵
Trail System
Grapevine Recreational Trails

Helped get the city to revitalize Grapevine Park and fund a new trail system. Now hosts SWKyMBA races, NICA league practices, and community volunteer days.

🛠️
Regional Trail Work
Ben Hawes, Harmonie & Cave Creek

Sustainable trail design and construction work across the region through SWKyMBA, an IMBA-affiliated chapter.

📍
County Initiative
Hopkins County Active Living Project

Helped lead the county-wide push toward active living, identifying trail and recreation land opportunities as the critical first step.
