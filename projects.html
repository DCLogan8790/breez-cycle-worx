Projects & Community Support

Here's How BReeZ Cycle Worx Supports Communities, Parks & Trail Systems.

Each of these is real work we've been part of — not just trails we ride, but communities and organizations we've supported along the way.

Community Initiative
Madisonville in Motion

Helping local leaders, riders, and community partners develop a long-term vision for outdoor recreation by leading the development of a Walk & Bike Master Plan, identifying trail opportunities, and creating connected pathways that encourage cycling, walking, and active transportation throughout the community.

BReeZ Support
  • Community meetings
  • Trail advocacy
  • Recreation planning
  • Local stakeholder support
Trail Support
Grapevine Recreation Area Trail System

Approximately 8.5 miles of natural surface trails designed for beginner and intermediate riders. The system includes dedicated flow lines, natural surface flowing singletrack, and connects multiple loop options — making it one of Western Kentucky's best places to begin mountain biking.

BReeZ Support
  • Trail assessments
  • Rider feedback
  • Community advocacy
  • Planning support
  • Ongoing maintenance collaboration
Regional Trail Support
Pennyrile Forest State Resort Park MTB Trails

Over 12 miles of natural surface mountain bike trails located within Pennyrile Forest State Resort Park. Riders experience wooded terrain, creek crossings, rolling climbs, and scenic forest views. The trail system offers an authentic Kentucky mountain biking experience suitable for families, recreational riders, traditional mountain bikes, and e-bikes where permitted.

BReeZ Support
  • Trail advocacy
  • Volunteer trail support
  • Community partnerships
  • Rider promotion
  • Regional recreation awareness
Regional Trail Support
Rudy Mines Trail — Ben Hawes Park

Approximately 8 miles of mountain bike trails featuring beginner and intermediate riding with flowing terrain, berms, optional jumps, and multiple loop combinations. A destination trail system serving riders throughout Western Kentucky.

BReeZ Support
  • Local rider feedback
  • Planning discussions
  • Volunteer support
  • Trail improvement recommendations
Trail Town Development
Dawson Springs Trail Town Revitalization

Supporting Dawson Springs as it strengthens its identity as a Kentucky Trail Town by improving rider navigation, community connections, and access between downtown and Pennyrile Forest.

BReeZ Support
  • Grant implementation support
  • Trail signage development
  • Community wayfinding
  • Visitor communication
  • Recreation planning

BReeZ Cycle Worx is a proud business member and sponsor of the Southwest Kentucky Mountain Bike Association (SWKyMBA), reinforcing our place in the local riding community and supporting the volunteer trail work happening across the region.

Community Planning Services

Need Help Getting Started?

Whether you're a city, county, parks department, neighborhood group, or trail advocate, BReeZ Cycle Worx can help during the early planning stages of outdoor recreation projects.

The goal isn't to replace an engineering firm or trail builder. It's to help communities understand what's possible, connect with the right partners, and move projects from ideas toward reality.
