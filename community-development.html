Community Development

Building Trails That Communities Believe In.

Every trail starts with a vision. But successful trail systems require much more than dirt and equipment — community support, city leadership, funding, stakeholder engagement, economic planning, and long-term maintenance. That's where we help.

Start a Conversation See Our Projects

Our Approach

Well-Built Isn't Enough. Trails Have to Be Well-Supported.

We work alongside city officials, parks departments, tourism organizations, local businesses, property owners, and riders to create trail systems that improve health, attract visitors, and become assets communities are proud to invest in.

Because healthy trails aren't just well-built. They're well-supported — by funding, by leadership, and by the community that uses them.
