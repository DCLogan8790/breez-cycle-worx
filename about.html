About Us

More Than Riders. We're Professionals.

We understand that successful trail systems aren't built with passion alone. They're built through planning, stakeholder engagement, business strategy, community support, and long-term sustainability.

Our Story

We're Riders First. Professionals Always.

BReeZ Cycle Worx began because we love riding bikes. Like many people, we started by spending weekends exploring trails throughout Western Kentucky. The more we rode, the more we realized something — great trail systems don't happen by accident.

Behind every successful trail network is a partnership between riders, community advocates, city leadership, businesses, tourism organizations, and long-term planning. That's where we found our purpose.

Today, BReeZ brings together professionals with backgrounds in business leadership, community development, engineering, project management, marketing, and trail design. We understand both sides of the conversation — the rider who wants better trails and the city leader responsible for investing public resources wisely. We believe successful trail systems create healthier communities, stronger local economies, and outdoor experiences that last for generations.

Our Team

People Who've Actually Built Trails.

Stephanie Camille
Director, Community Development

Stephanie's passion for bikes has grown into a career building projects that enhance community health and encourage active living. She holds an M.Ed in Community Development from Vanderbilt University.

She's worked with a wide range of businesses, state agencies, and nonprofits — including creating the Tennessee Conference on Volunteerism and Community Service as deputy director of Volunteer Tennessee, and conducting a health impact assessment for Nashville's Metro Planning Organization 30-year Bicycle and Pedestrian Plan.

  • M.Ed, Community Development — Vanderbilt
  • Former Deputy Director, Volunteer Tennessee
  • Nashville MPO Bike & Ped Plan — HIA
Richard Dixon
Trail Design & Senior Trail Builder

Richard has nearly two decades of mountain bike trail design and construction experience. Mentored early in his career by one of the Midwest's leading trail builders, he developed a strong foundation in sustainable trail design and rider-focused trail systems.

As a longtime member and volunteer with the Southwest Kentucky Mountain Bike Association (an IMBA chapter), Richard has partnered with government agencies, municipalities, and land managers to consult, secure funding, design, build, and maintain sustainable trail systems. His project experience includes Ben Hawes, Harmonie, Cave Creek, Grapevine Lake, Hanson, and other outdoor recreation initiatives.

  • SWKyMBA / IMBA Chapter Member
  • 20+ Years Trail Design & Construction
  • Ben Hawes · Harmonie · Cave Creek · Grapevine · Hanson
James Leslie Seargent
Owner & Founder — 20+ Years in Executive Leadership & Business Development

James brings 20+ years of executive leadership in business development, dealer network management, and product commercialization — building and managing partner networks across North America and Europe throughout his career. He holds a Bachelor of Science from Southern Illinois University.

After moving back to Madisonville, James brought that same leadership approach to a hands-on push for better trails and recreation in Hopkins County — helping identify land opportunities for hiking and biking as the critical first step. In 2020, he helped execute Madisonville in Motion, getting the city to revitalize Grapevine Park and fund a new trail system built by Rogue Trails of Arkansas. Grapevine Recreational Trails has since hosted the SWKyMBA local Time Trial race, NICA middle and high school mountain bike league practices, and community volunteer days with local partners like Carhartt and Corner Stone schools.

  • Founder, BReeZ Cycle Worx
  • 20+ Years Executive & Dealer Network Leadership
  • Partner Networks Across N. America & Europe
