About Us

More Than Riders. We're Professionals.

We understand that successful trail systems aren't built with passion alone. They're built through planning, stakeholder engagement, business strategy, community support, and long-term sustainability.

Our Story

We're Riders First. Professionals Always.

BReeZ Cycle Worx began because we love riding bikes. Like many people, we started by spending weekends exploring trails throughout Western Kentucky. The more we rode, the more we realized something — great trail systems don't happen by accident.

Behind every successful trail network is a partnership between riders, community advocates, city leadership, businesses, tourism organizations, and long-term planning. That's where we found our purpose.

Today, BReeZ brings together professionals with backgrounds in business leadership, community development, engineering, project management, marketing, and trail design. We understand both sides of the conversation — the rider who wants better trails and the city leader responsible for investing public resources wisely. We believe successful trail systems create healthier communities, stronger local economies, and outdoor experiences that last for generations.
